About The Kaavish

काविश — Soul in Semi-Classical Harmony

Formed in 1998 by Jaffer Zaidi (lead vocals, acoustic piano/harmonium) and Maaz Maudood (backing vocals, acoustic guitar), The Kaavish represents one of the most sublime and poignant chapters in contemporary South Asian semi-classical music.

Their debut studio album Gunkali (named after the morning raag) introduced an introspective, acoustic sound world that bridges classical Indian raags with Western piano ballads. With evergreen tracks like Faasle, Bachpan, Nindiya Re, and Tere Pyaar Mein, their compositions linger like a quiet cup of chai on a rainy afternoon.

Inspired by the aesthetic atmosphere of Deluxe Saloon (deluxesalon.in), this web app is a non-commercial tribute radio created for fans who seek sanctuary in the soothing melodies of Kaavish.
